## About the programme

The **Aster Ridge Foundation — Emerging Scholars Grant (2027 cohort)** supports early-career researchers in computing, engineering and environmental science with a two-year stipend and structured mentorship.

The programme is coordinated by **Shirley Nelson**, Programme Coordinator, Aster Ridge Foundation.
